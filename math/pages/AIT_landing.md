You've found my landing page for *algorithmic information theory* (AIT).

At its core, algorithmic information theory seeks to formalize notions of randomness, information content, and even logical reasoning capabilities. 

And, most fruitfully, AIT has been applied to *geometric measure theory* (GMT): refining classic results about fractal dimension and measure via counting and computability arguments.
